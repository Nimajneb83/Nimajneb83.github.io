Benjamin is the lab director and Assistant Professor of Cognitive Psychology in the [Department of Psychological & Brain Sciences](https://www.umass.edu/psychological-brain-sciences/) (starting Fall 2026). Before joining the UMass faculty, he earned a BS in cognitive neuroscience from Brown University, a PhD in cognitive psychology from the University of Chicago, and completed postdoctoral fellowships at UC Berkeley and the Institute for Advanced Study in Toulouse. He studies how cognition is constructed by experience.
